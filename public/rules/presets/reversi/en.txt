1.1 Reversi is played by two players, Black and White. Black moves first.
1.2 When the game ends, the player with more discs of their color on the board wins.
2.1 Play on an 8×8 board; discs go inside the squares. Each disc is black on one side and white on the other.
2.2 Start with four discs in the center squares placed diagonally: white on the top-left and bottom-right, black on the top-right and bottom-left.
3.1 Players take turns placing one disc with their color facing up on an empty square.
3.2 The new disc must trap at least one of the opponent's discs in a straight line (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) between itself and another of your discs, with no empty squares in between.
3.3 All trapped opposing discs are flipped to your color.
3.4 A single move can trap discs in several directions at once; flip the trapped discs in every direction.
3.5 If you have no move that traps an opposing disc, you must pass and your opponent moves again.
3.6 You may not pass if you have a legal move.
4.1 The game ends when neither player can move or the board is full.
4.2 Count the discs. The player with more discs wins; equal counts are a draw.
