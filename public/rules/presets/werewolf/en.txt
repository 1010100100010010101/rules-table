1.1 These rules summarize a common 12-player setup; they are not official rules. A moderator who does not play runs the game.
1.2 Players are split into the village team and the werewolf team. The village team includes villagers and power roles.
1.3 The village team wins when every werewolf has been exiled or killed.
1.4 The werewolves win when every villager is out, or every power role is out.
2.1 Roles for 12 players: 4 werewolves, 4 villagers, and 1 each of Seer, Witch, Hunter, and Guard.
2.2 The moderator shuffles the role cards and each player draws one, looking at it privately.
2.3 You may not show your role card to anyone while you are still in the game.
3.1 The game alternates between night and day, starting with the first night.
3.2 At night everyone closes their eyes. The moderator wakes each role with a night power in turn; that player opens their eyes, acts with gestures, and closes their eyes again.
3.3 Night order: Guard, werewolves, Seer, Witch.
4.1 On the first night the werewolves see who their teammates are. Each night the werewolves together choose one player to kill; if they disagree, the majority decides.
4.2 Each night the Seer checks one player, and the moderator signals whether that player is a werewolf or on the village team.
4.3 The Witch has one healing potion and one poison, each usable once.
4.4 The moderator shows the Witch who the werewolves killed that night; the Witch may use the healing potion to save that player.
4.5 The Witch may use the poison to kill one player. She may not use both potions on the same night.
4.6 The Witch may save herself only on the first night.
4.7 Each night the Guard protects one player, who cannot be killed by the werewolves that night. The Guard may not protect the same player two nights in a row.
4.8 If the same player is both protected by the Guard and saved by the Witch on the same night, that player still dies.
4.9 When the Hunter is out, they may shoot one player, who is also out. A Hunter killed by the Witch's poison may not shoot.
5.1 At dawn the moderator announces who died during the night, without saying how.
5.2 Players who die on the first night may give last words; players who die on later nights may not.
5.3 During the day, each living player speaks once, in turn.
5.4 After everyone has spoken, players vote to exile one player. The player with the most votes is out and gives last words.
5.5 If the top vote is tied, the tied players each speak again and everyone else votes again; if it is still tied, no one is exiled.
5.6 A werewolf may reveal themselves during the day. That werewolf is out, the day ends immediately, and there is no vote that day.
6.1 Players who are out may not speak or give hints to living players by expression or gesture.
6.2 The moderator checks for a win after each exile and at the end of each night.
