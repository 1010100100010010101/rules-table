1.1 Players are night-market vendors who run stalls to earn coins.
1.2 When any player has 30 coins, the game ends at the end of that round. The player with the most coins wins.
2.1 Each player takes 1 cart pawn and 10 coins.
2.2 Shuffle the stall cards and place them face down as a deck. Shuffle the event cards into a separate deck.
2.3 The youngest player goes first, then play continues clockwise.
3.1 Each turn, in order: roll and move, resolve the space, and optionally open a stall.
3.2 After your turn, the player on your left takes their turn.
4.1 On your turn, roll one die and move your cart clockwise that many spaces.
4.2 If you roll a 6, you may immediately roll one extra time. The extra roll does not trigger this rule again.
4.3 If you land on another player's stall, pay its owner the amount shown on that stall. If you don't have enough coins, pay all your coins; you don't go into debt.
5.1 Each player may own at most 3 stalls.
5.2 If you land on an empty lot, you may pay the price on a stall card to open a stall on that space.
5.3 Once a stall opens, it stays open until the end of the game.
6.1 If you land on an event space, draw an event card and resolve it immediately.
6.2 Typhoon card: no stall collects rent this round.
6.3 Night Market Festival card: the player who draws it gains 5 coins.
