1.1 Gomoku is played by two players, Black and White. Black moves first.
1.2 The first player to make an unbroken line of five stones of their color, horizontally, vertically, or diagonally, wins.
2.1 Play on a 15×15 board. Stones are placed on the intersections.
2.2 The board starts empty.
3.1 Players take turns placing one of their stones on any empty intersection.
3.2 Once placed, stones never move and are never captured.
3.3 You may not pass.
4.1 A line of exactly five or more than five stones (an overline) both count as a win.
4.2 If the board fills up and no one has five in a row, the game is a draw.
5.1 These rules use no forbidden moves. If your table wants tournament rules (for example, Black may not make double-threes, double-fours, or overlines), make them house rules before you start.
