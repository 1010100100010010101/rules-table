1.1 These rules summarize common play; they are not the official rulebook. 2 to 10 players.
1.2 The first player to get rid of all their cards wins the round.
2.1 The deck has 108 cards in four colors (red, yellow, green, blue). Each color has number cards 0 to 9 plus Skip, Reverse, and Draw Two action cards.
2.2 There are also 4 black Wild cards and 4 Wild Draw Four cards.
2.3 Shuffle and deal 7 cards to each player. The rest go face down as the draw pile.
2.4 Turn over the top card of the draw pile to start the discard pile. If it is a Wild Draw Four, put it back and turn over another.
2.5 The player to the dealer's left goes first, and play continues clockwise.
3.1 On your turn, play a card that matches the top card of the discard pile by color, number, or symbol.
3.2 You may instead play a Wild card and choose the next color.
3.3 If you can't or don't want to play, draw one card. If that card can be played, you may play it right away; otherwise play passes to the next player.
3.4 When the draw pile runs out, keep the top discard and shuffle the rest of the discard pile into a new draw pile.
4.1 Skip: the next player loses their turn.
4.2 Reverse: the direction of play reverses. With two players, Reverse works like Skip.
4.3 Draw Two: the next player draws 2 cards and loses their turn.
4.4 Wild: the player who plays it chooses the color.
4.5 Wild Draw Four: the player who plays it chooses the color, and the next player draws 4 cards and loses their turn.
4.6 You may play a Wild Draw Four only if you have no card matching the color of the top discard.
4.7 A player hit by a Wild Draw Four may challenge it. The player who played it shows their hand to the challenger; if it was played illegally, that player draws 4 instead; if it was legal, the challenger draws 6.
4.8 These rules do not allow stacking Draw Two or Draw Four cards onto the next player; if your table wants stacking, make it a house rule before you start.
5.1 When you play your second-to-last card and have one card left, you must say "UNO."
5.2 If you don't say "UNO" and another player catches you before the next player plays, you draw 2 cards as a penalty.
6.1 The round ends when someone plays their last card. The cards left in the other players' hands are scored for the winner.
6.2 Scoring: number cards are worth their face value; Skip, Reverse, and Draw Two are 20 points each; Wild and Wild Draw Four are 50 points each.
6.3 The first player to reach 500 points wins the game. You can also play a single round, where the first player out wins.
